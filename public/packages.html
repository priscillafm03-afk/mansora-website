Simple, transparent pricing.

What does the package fee cover?

Professional time, agreed deliverables, routine client communication and scheduled reporting or advisory support within the scope of the package you choose.

What can change the fee?

Transaction volume, number of employees, entities or branches, complexity, reporting frequency, tax history and any special assignments. Pricing is tailored to each client's needs and the scope of services required.

How do we get started?

A short discovery and scoping conversation confirms your needs, the right package, onboarding requirements and your final engagement fee. You receive your final quote after scoping.

Is extra work charged separately?

Audits, major historical clean-ups, complex tax investigations, valuations or specialist projects can be quoted separately where required.

Can you help if our records are incomplete or we're starting from scratch?

Yes. Our bookkeeping support covers businesses starting from scratch as well as businesses with incomplete financial records.

Are the packages affordable for small businesses?

Yes. Our package fees are designed as accessible starting points for Ghanaian SMEs and growing organisations, while reflecting the breadth of professional support.
