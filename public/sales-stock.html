Sales & Stock brings Mansora’s focus on financial clarity to the day-to-day running of a business. Core workflows are implemented and tested in software; early access is the next step towards learning with real shops.

From the counter to the close

The details that keep
a business moving.

Connected workflows for the people selling, the people managing stock and the owner keeping an eye on it all.

01 / SELL

A smoother sale

Barcode checkout and receipts, with offline selling in the desktop edition. Record cash, card and mobile-money payments alongside each sale.

Payment recording tracks how a customer paid; it does not process a card or mobile-money transaction.

02 / STOCK

See what is on the shelf

Manage products, carton costing, stock counts and purchases. Spot low stock and understand what is coming in and going out.

03 / CUSTOMERS

Keep customer credit clear

Keep customer records, record credit sales and track repayments, so balances have a history you can follow.

04 / DAILY CONTROL

A more confident close

Track shifts, cash movements and expenses. Reconcile the expected balance with the cash counted and review differences.

05 / REPORTS

Turn records into a clearer view

Review sales, payments, profit and stock reports. Export records for follow-up and analysis beyond the counter.

06 / YOUR TEAM

The right access for each role

Set staff permissions and use manager approvals for sensitive actions. Give people the tools they need with clear responsibilities.

Will it work without internet?

Offline selling is part of the desktop edition. The current hosted browser version requires internet. Cloud sync and service communications also require connectivity.

What has been verified?

The features described here are implemented and covered by software tests. Real-shop pilots and physical printer, scanner and cash-drawer acceptance are still to be completed. Desktop installers are not yet signed. No live payment-provider approval is claimed.

What happens after I join?

We save your request separately from the newsletter and contact you about early access when a suitable pilot place is available. Joining does not create an account, send a tester invitation, reserve a place or start a subscription. Pricing and timing will be discussed before you commit.
